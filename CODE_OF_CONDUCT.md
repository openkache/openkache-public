# Code of Conduct

OpenKache is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free
community. Everyone who participates in the project is expected to follow this
policy.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other people with respect and empathy.
- Discuss ideas, evidence, and tradeoffs without attacking the person presenting
  them.
- Give constructive feedback, ask questions in good faith, and make room for
  people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Respect personal boundaries, privacy, and requests to stop unwelcome
  behavior.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize when appropriate, and correct
  harmful behavior.
- Protect confidential information, including private reports, credentials,
  personal data, and non-public security details.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, stalking, or sustained
  unwanted contact.
- Insults, personal attacks, derogatory comments, or deliberate humiliation.
- Sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome sexual attention, or advances.
- Publishing or threatening to publish another person's private information
  without permission.
- Trolling, spam, impersonation, knowingly misleading claims, or deliberate
  disruption of project work.
- Malicious technical activity, attempts to gain unauthorized access, or
  disclosure intended to expose people or systems to harm.
- Retaliation against someone for making a good-faith report or participating
  in its review.
- Knowingly false or malicious conduct reports. A good-faith report that cannot
  be substantiated is not a violation.

## Scope

This policy applies in all OpenKache project spaces, including repositories,
issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, chat, meetings, events, and
other project communications. It also applies when someone represents
OpenKache in public or communicates directly with another person about project
work.

The policy covers both public and private interactions connected to the
project. It does not regulate conduct in a person's private life that is
unrelated to OpenKache.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to
[conduct@openstd.com](mailto:conduct@openstd.com). Do not post
sensitive incident details in a public issue or discussion.

Share the information you are comfortable providing. Useful details can
include what happened, when and where it happened, who was involved, links or
screenshots, witnesses, and any immediate safety concern. If the report
involves a maintainer, say so; that maintainer will not participate in the
enforcement decision.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Information
may be shared with people needed to review and respond to the report, or when
required by law or an immediate safety concern. Confidentiality may limit what
maintainers can disclose about their review or its outcome.

## Enforcement

OpenKache maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this
policy. They will consider the seriousness and impact of the behavior, its
context, whether it is repeated, and the person's response when deciding what
action is proportionate.

Possible actions include:

1. A private correction or warning that identifies the unacceptable behavior
   and the change required.
2. Removal or editing of content, or pausing, locking, or closing a
   conversation.
3. Temporary limits on participation or contact.
4. A temporary suspension from project spaces.
5. A permanent ban from project spaces.

Maintainers may combine actions or skip steps when the severity, pattern, or
immediate risk warrants it. Enforcement decisions should focus on protecting
people and the project, stopping harmful behavior, and setting clear conditions
for any continued participation.

A person subject to an enforcement action may request reconsideration by
emailing [conduct@openstd.com](mailto:conduct@openstd.com) with relevant
context. A maintainer involved in the original incident will not decide that
request.
